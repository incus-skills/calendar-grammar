3. **The home culture acts**: Group A applies its own established, culturally accepted penalties—the full force of its own system—to the perpetrator.

4. **Accountability is absolute**: The perpetrator's community cannot claim the punishment is illegitimate or foreign. It is their own standard, applied to their own member.

5. **Feuds are prevented**: Because the home culture has policed its own rigorously, Group B has no legitimate grievance. The cycle of retaliation is broken.
