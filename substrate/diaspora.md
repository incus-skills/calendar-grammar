### 2. The VAT Barrier: "Open Market Value" on Barter

    1.  The group only claims **exact reimbursement** of costs (no profit margin).
    2.  The services are directly necessary for the members' exempt or non-business activities (or can be argued as such in a mutual context).
    3.  The arrangement does not distort competition.
  - **Application:** Instead of "licensing IP for credits," the federation is structured as a **Cost Sharing Group**.
