# Dog training as didactic othopraxis (education system in microcism)

Pre-comming of age application of the disciplines to the development of an external mind.

Overlaping dog ownership, the first trained dogs interaction with the next demonstrates the difference between the dynamics of an isolated entrainment and an interleaved one.

Overlap softens the impact of loss, each dog is only a 10 year cycle.

## Recursively structured training program

A dog learns from interactions it has with its environment.[3] This can be through classical conditioning, where it forms an association between two stimuli; non-associative learning, where its behavior is modified through habituation or sensitisation; and operant conditioning, where it forms an association between an antecedent and its consequence.

All the approaches work but range from `horse breaking` to `horse whispering` and within the context of MFHIS we compose a program from the affordances of the available methods to manufacture a re-entry opportunity for othopraxis for the trainer and structured training for the dog where the dog is another substrate for the ubiquitous expression of the disciplines/OE.
