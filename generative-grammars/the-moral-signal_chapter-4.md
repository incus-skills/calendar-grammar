natural selection works by producing signaling systems that are extremely leaky both on the sender side and on the receiver side. Senders evolve to speak too much, receivers evolve to listen too much, the only thing that can refine such a system is if the junk information turns out to be repeated enough and disadvantagious enough to cause evolutionary forces that will limit the leaks. Most of the time it doesn't because words are cheap.

Misfirings are omnipresent in the world of signals. One reason it is so hard for leaky senders and receiver to improve is that the vast majority of signalling interference that occurs in our society are costless misfirings.

Rule number 5. - No costly signaling systems are the subject of greater information leaks and control leaks and will fail to converge toward optimum states through evolutionary time. By optimal I mean a system that is as efficient and as specialized as possible to play its function. Human language and human culture, because they are so easy to produce and consume are subject to rule number 5 and are therefore unlikely to be efficient endevors any time soon.

This is why they fit sturgeon law. that 90% of everything is crap. This is the case because the calorific cost superfluous signaling through speech and art production is minimal and probably evolutionarily meaningless.
