## The Ritual Sequence as Re-entry Protocol

### Phase 1: Preparation (Nyasa)

> **Function:** Placement of the mantra's syllables on the body, aligning the observer with the operational field.

**Procedure:**

- Touch the feet for "Tat," the ankles for "Sa," the calves for "Vi," etc.
- This is the observer-position calibration layer.

### Phase 2: Pranayama (Breath Control)

> **Function:** Regulation of life force; preparation for re-entry.

**Procedure:** Pūraka (inhalation), Kumbhaka (retention), Rechaka (exhalation) in a 1:3:2 ratio.

### Phase 3: Karanyasa and Anganyasa (Hand and Limb Placement)

> **Function:** Encoding the operational grammar into the observer's body.

**Procedure:** Touch specific fingers and limbs with specific mantras.

### Phase 4: Gayatri Avahana (Invocation)

> **Function:** Re-entry into the operational field.

**Procedure:** Invoke Gayatri in her three forms: Brahmavādinī (morning), Rudravādinī (afternoon), Vishnuvādinī (evening).

### Phase 5: 24 Mudrās

> **Function:** Gestural enaction of the 24-syllable grammar; the full operational sequence.

**Procedure:** Perform the 24 mudrās while reciting the mantra.

### Phase 6: Japa (Recitation)

> **Function:** Deepening the re-entry; entrainment.

**Procedure:** Recite the mantra 108 times or more.

### Phase 7: Suryopasthanam / Varunopasthanam

> **Function:** Completion; grounding the re-entry.

**Procedure:** Salute the Sun God (morning/afternoon) or Varuna (evening).
