## The hormonal mechanism

The research shows that **stress and false signals directly disrupt the HPG axis**:

**In other words:**

- The **primary reproductive signal** requires **low stress, high oxytocin, stable dopamine** to function.

- The result is **reproductive dysregulation** — not just behavioral, but **hormonal and physiological**.
