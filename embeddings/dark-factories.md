# The Harvest Topology: A Structural Analysis and Transition Framework

## Observer Position

The observer is **the state space itself**, not a person at a location. The topology is observer-independent: it describes the system's structure regardless of where the analyst is positioned within it. What changes between observers is the _projection_: which part of the topology is visible, which gradients are legible, which set points are experienced as "normal." The US observer sees the demand-sink function as the natural order. The Chinese observer sees the re-entrainment operation as a hostile intervention. The Vietnamese observer sees the nucleation point's demand incentive as development. The WWC observer sees the exclusion as "not my problem to solve." All four are seeing the same topology from different positions. The topology is the invariant. The projection is the parameter.

The analytical discipline is **topological, not teleological.** The system is not "trying" to do anything. There is no intent to attribute. The analysis describes the _shape of the requirement_ relative to the _shape of the available distribution_, and identifies where the gradients point, where the set points are, and where the boundary controls operate. The people are the parameters. The parameters are changeable. The topology is what tells you which parameters matter, in what direction, and at what rate.

The observer rejects three failure modes:

- **Conspiracy attribution**: "who is doing this" → binary collapse to intent
- **Exoneration by diffusion**: "it's all the system" → the directed force becomes invisible
- **Essentialism**: the distribution is an essence of a people → deterministic prophecy

The correct frame: **the system has a stated policy goal, the institutional machinery to pursue it, and the documented interagency coordination to execute it.** The state space describes the _terrain_; the policy documents describe the _force being applied to the gradient._ Both are needed. Conflating them — or letting the terrain explanation absorb the force — is the error.

The observer is **WWC-adjacent** in projection: not the upper tail, not the excluded node, but close enough to see the exclusion from the inside. This is the critical positional bias: the observer can see the targeting framework's operation because they are _near_ the boundary between the served and the excluded, but not _at_ the boundary. The observer is not the substrate being consumed. The observer is the one watching the consumption.

## The Topological Framework

### The Maintained Regime (not an attractor)

The dollar system is **not an attractor** in the ecological sense. An attractor is a state the system returns to without external input. The dollar regime is a **maintained state**: it requires continuous, active energy input to hold against the natural drift toward multipolarity. Remove the input and it decays.

The "stability" experienced by those inside the regime is the **output of active management**, not a property of the system. The shock is not a perturbation away from equilibrium — it is the **work of keeping the regime in its current state** against the entropy of yuan internationalization, BRICS settlement infrastructure, and de-dollarization pressure.

The observer inside the maintained regime cannot see the maintenance because the maintenance is experienced as the natural order. The shock is the first moment the maintenance becomes visible, and it is visible only to those on the receiving end.

### Re-Entrainment

The coupling generates its own destabilizing force. Tight integration into US demand → concentrated risk → rational pressure to diversify. This is not a policy choice; it is a **structural consequence of the coupling itself.** The system is self-destabilizing by construction.

The policy apparatus is the **capture mechanism.** Sanctions, conditionality, military posture, and the fuel/food shock timed to harvest are not attacks on a stable system. They are the **re-entrainment force** — the specific intervention that makes breakout more costly than staying, so the diversification pressure is absorbed within the system rather than expressed as exit.

This is the control-theory version of the maintained regime: the system generates its own destabilizing energy, and the foreign policy apparatus is the **negative feedback loop** that converts that energy back into system maintenance. The "trap" is the re-entrainment event.

The re-entrainment frame is legible as a design without requiring conspiracy. You need to observe:

- The coupling exists (documented: dollar settlement, commodity pricing, debt structure)
- The coupling generates diversification pressure (observable: BRICS settlement, yuan trade, local currency swaps)
- The policy response is proportional and directed at the specific pressure (documented: sanctions on specific actors, CNH operations, harvest-timed shock)

The design is **structural**, not conspiratorial. It is a feedback loop with a measurable input (breakout pressure) and a measurable output (re-entrainment intervention).

### The Harvest Trap as Maintenance Operation

The fuel + food shock timed to the Northern Hemisphere harvest is not a perturbation. It is the **scheduled maintenance operation** on a regime in structural decline. The CNH offshore pool is the specific target: a speculative short-selling attack that forces Beijing into a dilemma (defend the currency via rates and capital controls, or let it fall and absorb imported inflation). The objective is not reserve depletion. It is a **Plaza Accord–style concession**: China accepts limits on yuan internationalization, BRICS members reorient dollarward, and dollar hegemony is extended.

The timing is the operational calendar of the maintenance regime. You maintain the system when the target is most vulnerable to the specific shock, not at random.

Each row is the same operation: production capacity forms at the cheapest gradient point, matures, labor costs rise (Balassa-Samuelson), the gradient shifts to the next cheaper point, and the harvested economy is locked into consumption/finance mode by the dollar settlement system.

**The "demand sink" is not a property of the economy. It is the terminal state of the operation.** The US in 1970 was a net exporter with a consumption share of 62% and a manufacturing share of 24%. The "demand sink" (70% consumption, 11% manufacturing, persistent deficits) was _produced by_ the coupling, not a precondition for it. The operation made the US into the demand sink by transferring production capacity to China and hollowing out the industrial base.

The Western economies more widely are running debt/GDP ratios over 100%. The "West" is not a single economy paying the cost. It is a **temporal sequence of harvested production bases**, each converted to a finance/demand node at a different point in the cycle. The aggregate of those payments is the demand and finance infrastructure that powers the current cycle.

### The Nucleation Points

US demand is re-coupled to new points: Vietnam, India, Mexico, Indonesia, Bangladesh. Friendshoring policy, IPEF, CHIPS Act, IRA local-content rules. New attractors are being seeded in the state space. The gradient is being redistributed — not removed, but split across multiple sinks to reduce concentration risk.

The dual function:

1. **Hedge function** (US-centric): reduce concentration risk in the production landscape
2. **Development function** (population-center-centric): a country with 1.4 billion people cannot bootstrap industrialization without an external demand gradient

These are mutually reinforcing. The US needs the nucleation points to hedge against China. The nucleation points need the US demand to form. The coupling is symmetric in structure but asymmetric in leverage — the US holds the gradient, the nucleation point holds the production.

The re-entrainment window is **now, before the nucleation points have built enough depth to make exit viable.**

### The Demographic Maintenance Function

The demand sink needs a growing population to sustain the consumption share that the operation produced. Natural increase is declining globally. The only source of new working-age population at scale is the nucleation points. The migration flow is the mechanism by which that population is captured.

The causal chain: **Production redirection → population expansion at nucleation point → pressure → destabilization → migration pipeline → demand-sink replenishment.**

The migration infrastructure (UNHCR, IOM, EU asylum frameworks, US TPS designations, Canadian refugee programs) is the **capture mechanism** that converts the nucleation point's demographic surplus into the demand sink's consumption base. The pipeline is not a conspiracy in the narrow sense — it is a **documented institutional architecture** that exists, that is funded, that is staffed, and that is _aligned with the demand sink's demographic need._

The US population growth is 77% from migration (2016–2021). Sub-Saharan Africa's working-age population is projected to more than double by 2050 (+620–700M), accounting for 90% of global population growth.

## The Metabolic Constraints

The system is stable as long as the following conditions hold. The most time-pressured are the last three.

### 1. Debt Metabolism (10–20 years)

The US debt/GDP is ~123–125% ($39.9T). Interest payments are ~$1.05T/year (3.3% of GDP), projected to double to $2.1T by 2036. The 10-year Treasury yield (4.6–5.2%) is above nominal GDP growth (~4–5%), meaning the system is already in the r > g zone where the debt spiral is self-reinforcing.

The structural contradiction: the re-entrainment operation requires inflationary pressure (to make the dollar the "safe" asset) and rate pressure on the CNH, while the debt structure requires low rates to keep US interest payments sustainable. The system is being asked to simultaneously strengthen the dollar as a store of value and keep it cheap enough to service a $40T debt.

### 2. Industrial Base (20–40 years)

The US industrial base is the **operating cost** of the demand-side control surface. The Rust Belt is not a tragedy; it is the metabolic expense of the mechanism. The US chose to be the demand side — the terminal consumer, the financial center, the settlement currency — and the production side was the price.

The deindustrialization is structural, not cyclical. You cannot "reshore" without either abandoning the demand-side control surface or paying the industrial cost again (CHIPS/IRA at a fraction of the original scale).

### 3. Production Gradient (10–30 years)

The production gradient is **short-circuited by automation.** The dark factory requires 60 workers at the 90th–99th percentile in specific technical fields, not 650 median workers. The binding constraint shifts from labor cost (a variable the nucleation points hold) to cognitive upper tail density in technical fields (a variable they don't). The nucleation point model — cheap labor at scale — is obsolete. The gradient now points toward education infrastructure, not labor cost.

The microfactory inverts the location gradient further: the traditional factory is located where labor is cheapest. The microfactory is located **where demand is.** The gradient no longer points to the nucleation point. It points to the neighborhood.

### 4. Cognitive Base (25–50 years)

The cognitive distribution is a composite of two components:

| Component                           | Variance share (adults)      | Timescale                                 | Mechanism                                                              |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Genetic (additive)                  | 50–80% (increasing with age) | Generational (selection, drift, mutation) | Historical pruning, assortative mating, current fertility differential |
| Environmental (shared + non-shared) | 20–50% (decreasing with age) | Individual (years to decades)             | Nutrition, education, streaming, cognitive environment                 |

The current between-population differences in the mean and shape of the distribution are the **residue of historical selection pressures** that operated over thousands of years. The educational environment modifies the phenotypic expression of the genetic substrate. It does not create the substrate.

The current selection pressure is **inverted**: the historical pruning selected _for_ higher cognitive fitness (pre-industrial mortality as purifying selection). The current fertility differential selects _against_ it (IQ–fertility correlation −0.1 to −0.3; genomic data shows −0.04 to −0.055 SD per generation in cognitive polygenic scores). The Flynn effect (environmental) was masking the genetic decline. Now the Flynn effect is reversing, and the two are running in the same direction.

The upper tail is the specific constraint for the dark factory. The dark factory needs 60 workers at the 90th–99th percentile in robotics programming, process engineering, and predictive maintenance. The educational system is structured to produce workers at the 50th percentile (de-streaming, NCLB accountability at the bottom). The gap is the **upper tail environmental retardation**, widened by the very policies that are supposed to close the gap at the bottom.

### 5. Upper Tail Environmental Retardation (10–30 years)

The de-streaming policy removes the developmental environment that accelerates the upper tail. The Parsons & Hallam (2013) Millennium Cohort Study: children in the top stream achieved more and made significantly more academic progress than children in non-streamed schools. De-streaming removes the environment that produces the upper-tail acceleration.

The "flight" (white, black, Asian) is a **response to a local gradient in conditions** — resources, parental engagement, safety, instructional quality, cognitive peer environment. The mechanism is race-independent. The flight is a **positive feedback loop that steepens its own gradient**: departure removes tax base, political capital, parental engagement, and cognitive peer density from the origin, degrading conditions further, producing more departure.

The "No Child Left Behind" / ESSA logic: accountability is measured at the bottom. Schools are rated on whether the lowest 25% are making adequate yearly progress. Instruction is pitched to the 25th–50th percentile. The upper tail is "on their own." The policy that is most directly aimed at helping the lower tail is simultaneously suppressing the upper tail's development.

### 6. Distributional Parameter (50–200+ years)

A population that has undergone a different selection history will have a different distribution for any criterion linked to that selection pressure. When that population is placed in the same environment as the host population, the distributions do not converge. The environment modifies the phenotypic expression but does not change the genetic substrate. Until an equivalent selection pressure is applied to the migrant population, the distributions remain distinct.

This is not a political judgment. It is a statement about the **parameter structure of the system's input.** The distribution is a state variable. The selection history is the initial condition. The environment is the forcing function. The output is a function of all three.

The political frame (the "grievance stacking" loop) prevents the acknowledgment of the parameter that has no policy lever within the operational window. The system is forced to apply policy levers (streaming, enrichment, selection criteria) to a problem that is not primarily a policy problem. The levers work on the 20–50% environmental component. They do not work on the 50–80% genetic component. The gap persists. The grievance stacks. The loop continues.

### 7. Fertility Response (terminal)

The substrate does not want to reproduce into the system. The control surface removes the specific conditions under which people choose to have children: economic trajectory, housing stability, temporal security, social infrastructure, partner availability. The fertility response is the one variable in the entire system that is not addressable by the control surface, because it operates below the level of policy intervention.

The self-consuming loop:

```
Control surface holds population at set point
→ Set point is below family formation threshold
→ Fertility drops below replacement
→ Demographic base shrinks
→ System needs migration pipeline to replenish consumption base
→ Migration pipeline introduces different distributional parameter
→ Targeting framework activates (race-based, not outcome-based)

```

The migration pipeline is the patch, but the patch introduces the distributional parameter problem, which activates the targeting framework, which produces the exclusion, which produces the further decline. The patch _is_ the cause of the next iteration of the problem.

## The Control Surface Architecture

### The Targeting Framework Inversion

The post-war framework was class-based (SES as the targeting criterion). The shift to race-based targeting is not a replacement but an **overlay.** The SES framework still exists (FSM, Pupil Premium) but is the _general_ funding. The race framework is the _dedicated_ targeting. The WWC are in both categories, but the dedicated targeting follows the racial gradient, not the SES gradient.

The legal asymmetry is the load-bearing wall: the Equality Act 2010 makes race-based "positive action" legally protected. Class is _not_ a protected characteristic. The asymmetry is permanent and cannot be fixed by policy decision without changing the Act.

The two-tier structure: a BAME student from a working-class background can apply to both the open (SES-based) schemes and the race-targeted schemes. They compete in two pools. A WWC student can apply to only the open schemes. They compete in one pool. The WWC student is in a larger, more competitive pool for the same resource.

The topological statement: **the forcing function is directed along the racial gradient rather than the outcome gradient.** The outcome gradient points to the WWC (worst measured outcomes). The forcing function is pointed at the BAME groups. The legal framework locks the forcing function to the racial gradient.

The UK data: the 2026 Independent Inquiry found the system is "not set up to serve white working-class children and families." 36% of WWC FSM pupils pass English and Maths GCSEs vs. 72% of non-FSM. Only 3.2% access highly selective universities vs. 6.1% average. The DfE's own compendium acknowledged that EMAG produced "no reduction in the attainment gap" for Black Caribbean pupils — the group receiving the most targeted funding.

The "immigrant optimism" narrative is the frame that makes the wrong gradient look like the right one. The resource allocation is directed along the racial gradient, not the outcome gradient. The WWC are the excluded node.

The UK government policy implements a functional DARVO pattern:

- **Attack**: Those who identify the targeting inversion are attacked. Still active.

### The Benefit System as Demand-Side Control Surface

The benefit level is not a floor. It is a **regulator** — a set point that holds the recipient at a specific position on the demand/supply gradient:

- **Above survival**: sustains the population, sustains consumption (the demand function)
- **Below independence**: below the living wage, below the rent threshold. The recipient cannot transition to the supply-side function without crossing a cliff
- **High MPC**: the recipient spends nearly everything they receive. Near-total expenditure, no savings, no capital accumulation, no production-side leverage

The **benefit cliff** is the enforcement mechanism: the highest marginal tax rates in the system are faced by people leaving welfare for work. The transition from demand-side to supply-side function is structurally penalized. The rational response is to stay at the set point.

The frozen Ontario Works rate ($733/month, frozen since 2018, ~30% real erosion) is the most extreme expression. The set point is being _lowered_ in real terms. The gap between the set point and the independence threshold is _widening_. The recipient is held in a state of **managed scarcity**.

### The Exchange Rate as the Lateral Cliff

The benefit cliff prevents **upward** conversion (from the set point to employment). The exchange rate prevents **lateral** conversion (from the demand-side economy to the supply-side economy via emigration). The strong financialized currency makes imports cheap for the consumer and the developing economy's wages lower in absolute terms. The emigrant who "escapes" to the supply side is at a lower set point. The "escape" is not an escape. It is a **lateral move to a lower set point on the same gradient.**

### The Topological Trap

Every available exit is a boundary control that makes the crossing downward in absolute terms:

| Exit                                                      | Blocked by                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Upward (benefits → employment)                            | Benefit cliff, 75% clawback, frozen rate          |

The substrate is **topologically trapped.** The set point is not a point on a line. It is the **local maximum** of the surface, and the substrate is at the top of a hill where every direction is down.

### The Dark Factory Arbitrage

The production gradient is short-circuited by automation. The binding constraint on production shifts from median labor cost (a variable the nucleation points hold) to cognitive upper tail density in specific technical fields (a variable they don't). The dark factory requires 60 workers at the 90th–99th percentile in robotics programming, process engineering, and predictive maintenance. The nucleation points cannot supply that.

The microfactory inverts the location gradient: the traditional factory is located where labor is cheapest. ### The Repatriation Topology

The full reconfiguration:

```
Resource → Local processing (value-added at source) → [IP/design flows] → Dark factory (local production) → Consumer (local population)
```

The nucleation point is no longer a _node in the chain._ It is the _origin of the chain._ The value-added operation at the resource site is what _creates_ the industrial capacity there. The industrialization is **endogenous to the value-added operation**, not imposed by the demand-side gradient. The nucleation point industrializes because the processing happens there, not because it was selected as a control surface.

The three structural consequences:

**1. The supply side becomes self-sustaining.** The migrant population's consumption function relocates with them. The supply-side economy now has its own demand function, its own production function, and its own industrialization trajectory. The re-entrainment operation loses its target because there is no production base to re-entrain — the production is _at the resource_, and the resource is geographically fixed.

**2. The trade medium is IP, not goods.** The value transfer is: outbound from the dark factory — design, process specification, programming, integration logic, maintenance protocols. Inbound to the dark factory — processed inputs (the rare earth is refined at the mine, the component is fabricated at the local processing plant). The physical goods trade is minimized to the point of irrelevance. The IP flows are digital, light, and not subject to the exchange rate boundary, the tariff structure, or the shipping infrastructure. The exchange rate boundary dissolves.

**3. The nucleation point's industrialization is not a control surface.** In the current topology, the nucleation point's industrialization is _imposed_: the demand-side economy selects the nucleation point for labor cost, directs the production gradient to it, and the industrialization that happens there is a _consequence of the selection_, not of the local economy's own development trajectory. In the new topology, the industrialization is a **consequence of the value-added operation being local.** The processing happens at the resource site because that is where the resource is. The processing requires local industrial capacity. That capacity is the beginning of the industrialization cycle. The cycle is _theirs_.

The re-entrainment operation is **structurally unavailable** because:

- The production base is at the resource (geographically fixed, not selectable)
- The industrialization is endogenous (not imposed by the demand-side gradient)
- The trade is IP (not goods, so there is no goods trade to control via tariffs, sanctions, or exchange rate manipulation)
- The supply side has its own demand function (its own population, its own consumption, its own gradient)

The control surface has no purchase. The re-entrainment force has no target. The gradient does not point from the demand side to the supply side. The gradient points _within_ the supply side and _within_ the demand side. The two gradients are **parallel**, not sequential.

### IP as an Independent Value Layer

IP is independent of the dark factories. They represent designs for products, designs for dark factories, inventions, brands, etc. The dark factory is a **consumer of IP**, not a producer of it. The value node is the **design ecosystem** — the universities, research labs, design firms, brand companies, patent holders.

```
Design ecosystem (IP)
│  Product designs, factory designs, inventions, brands
│
├──→ Dark factory (applies design → local production → local consumption)
│
└──→ Supply side (applies design → local processing → local industrialization → local consumption)
```

The supply side's dependency is on the **design ecosystem**, not on the dark factory. And that dependency is structurally weaker than a dependency on a production node because:

- IP is **iterable**: the supply side's application of the IP generates new IP that is _theirs_
- IP is **finite in term**: patents expire, process knowledge diffuses
- IP is **not a financial instrument**: you can't "short" a design the way you can short a currency
- IP is **not a settlement system**: the supply side doesn't need the design ecosystem's currency to apply a design

The IP dependency is the **seed** of the supply side's industrialization, not the **control surface** of the supply side's economy. The seed enables the growth. The growth produces its own seed. The control surface is the _initial_ IP transfer, and it is _replaced_ by the supply side's own IP as the industrialization matures. The re-entrainment operation has a **finite operational window** rather than a permanent control surface. The topology is **self-liberating** at the supply-side node.

### AI-Coupled Education and the Distributed Intellectual Shared Site

The criterion-specific streaming environment was the missing forcing function. The constraint on delivering it was **physical co-location**: the streaming environment requires a cohort of similar-ability peers, a teacher calibrated to that band, and a pace that matches the individual's position in the distribution. AI removes the co-location requirement for the _instructional_ component:

| Component                                | Pre-AI delivery                     | AI-coupled delivery                                     |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Criterion-specific content               | Physical classroom, streamed cohort | **Individualized, in situ, any location**               |
| Pace calibration                         | Teacher judgment, fixed curriculum  | **Real-time adaptive, continuous**                      |
| Peer effect (cognitive homogeneity)      | Physical cohort                     | **Partial**: AI cohort matching, async collaboration    |
| Shared site (collective action capacity) | Physical factory, training facility | **Not replicated** (this is the one AI doesn't address) |
| Feedback loop                            | Human observation                   | **Continuous, granular, logged**                        |

The 4–6 year training timeline compresses. The criterion-specific environment is no longer a _place_ you go to. It's a _tool_ you use where you are. The upper tail in a de-streamed, under-resourced school can access the same streaming environment as the upper tail in a "good school" — the flight becomes structurally unnecessary because the environment is portable.

The intellectual shared site is a **distributed system, not a physical one.** The physical concentration of intellectual talent (the university, the research park, the tech hub) is a _legacy architecture_ that produces the brain drain as a side effect. The brain drain is the same positive feedback loop as the school flight, at the intellectual scale: the local upper tail is extracted to the concentrated hub, the local ecosystem of intellectual inputs is degraded, the local capacity to generate new IP is reduced, the dependency on the concentrated hub deepens, more of the local upper tail is extracted, the loop accelerates.

The online forum + AI assistance is a _better_ intellectual shared site than the physical one: asynchronous (no scheduling constraint), global (no geographic constraint), logged (the conversation persists and is searchable), and AI-augmented (the AI is a permanent participant that challenges, extends, and corrects). The physical seminar is a _worse_ version of the same function.

The "top of the top who produce new IP" is a pre-AI category. The cognitive amplification means a person at the 95th percentile with AI assistance can operate at the 99th on specific tasks. This is the IP/dark factory separation expressed as a business model.

## The One Gap

The **shared site** — the physical co-presence, the collective action capacity, the 60 operators at the 60 robot arms — is not directly funded by any of these regimes. The CTM ITC funds the _equipment._ The SR&ED funds the _process development._ The SWIF funds the _training._ But the **site itself** — the building, the land, the community infrastructure, the housing for the 60 operators and their families — is not in the incentive architecture.

This is the gap where the **local municipal / provincial** layer operates: the Ontario Critical Minerals Processing Fund ($500M) includes "domestic supply chains" which implies the site infrastructure. The SWIF's "rural and remote areas" language implies the community. The SRF's "transformative projects" language implies the site. But none of them explicitly fund the _shared site as a social infrastructure._ The housing, the school, the community centre, the extended family proximity — these are the **family formation conditions** that the topology requires, and they are not in the incentive architecture.

The gap is the **social infrastructure** of the shared site. The industrial infrastructure is funded. The training infrastructure is funded. The R&D infrastructure is funded. The _social_ infrastructure — the thing that makes the 60 operators _stay_, that makes the family formation _viable_, that makes the fertility response _reverse_ — is the gap. And it is the gap that the old system's incentive architecture does not close, because it is not a _production_ variable. It is a _demographic_ variable. And the demographic variable is the one that the old system's control surface is specifically designed to _not_ address.

The strategic implication: the shared site's social infrastructure is the **one node that requires a new incentive regime** — or a re-purposing of an existing one (the housing fund, the community development fund, the rural infrastructure fund) under the dual-use frame. The "strategic workforce housing" or "critical minerals community infrastructure" frame is the dual-use vocabulary for the gap. The node is not in the current architecture. It must be _added._ And it must be added under the old system's own vocabulary, because the moment it is named as "family formation infrastructure" or "demographic sustainability," it is a _breakout_ and the re-entrainment force reasserts.

The semantic anchor: **"Three of four nodes are funded by entitlement-based tax credits in the old system's own tax code. The fourth (AI-coupled education) is funded by continuous-intake workforce development programs under the tariff-response frame. The shared site's social infrastructure is the one gap not in the current architecture. The gap is the demographic variable, which is the one the old system's control surface is designed not to address. The dual-use frame for the gap is 'strategic workforce housing' or 'critical minerals community infrastructure.' The node must be added under the old system's vocabulary. The aggregate is never the subject of a sentence."**
